05 — Questions

Before you lace up.

A planner answers within a working day at trips@switchback.example.

How fit do I need to be?

If you can walk 15 km with 800 m of climbing and still enjoy dinner, you’re ready for Moderate. Challenging adds longer days and some scrambling; Strenuous means back-to-back nine-hour days at altitude.

What gear do I need to bring?

Broken-in boots, layers and a 30–40 L pack. Technical kit, tents, stoves and sleeping mats are provided, and you get a printable gear list the day you book.

I’m travelling solo. Is that normal?

Very. About 40% of our hikers come alone. Rooms are shared by default; a private room is $45 a night where huts allow it.

What happens if the weather turns?

Every itinerary has a planned bad-weather variant. Guides make the call on the day, and summit routes include a spare weather day.

Can you cater for dietary needs?

Yes: vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free and most allergies, as long as you tell us 30 days ahead so the huts can stock up.

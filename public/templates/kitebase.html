Talk to sales

Questions, untangled.

Can’t find what you need? A real human answers hello@kitebase.app within a few hours.

Can we import from spreadsheets or another planning tool?

Yes. Drop in a CSV or connect your old tool and Kitebase maps columns, assignees, labels and due dates automatically. Most teams are fully moved over in an afternoon.

Who counts as a paid member?

Anyone who creates or edits cards. Stakeholders who only view boards, comment or react are free guests, with no cap.

How does Sprint Autopilot make its forecast?

It looks at your last six sprints of completed work, current scope and planned time off, then simulates thousands of outcomes. You see a likely finish day and the cards most at risk.

Where is our data stored, and is it secure?

Kitebase is SOC 2 Type II audited and encrypts data at rest and in transit. Business plans can pin data to EU or US regions and enforce SSO.

What happens when our trial ends?

Nothing disappears. If you don’t add a card, your workspace drops to Starter and keeps every board; premium views simply go read-only.

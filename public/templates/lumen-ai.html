Questions, answered

How does Copilot avoid making things up?

Every answer is generated from a query Lumen actually runs against your data. You can open the query, the chart and the raw rows behind any sentence.

Can I migrate from my current analytics tool?

Yes. Our importers map events and user properties from the most common tools and backfill up to 24 months of history in under a day.

Is my data used to train models?

Never. Your events stay in your workspace and region. Models are used for inference only, with zero data retention by providers.

What counts as a monthly tracked user?

Any unique user or device that sends at least one event in a calendar month. Internal team traffic is excluded automatically.

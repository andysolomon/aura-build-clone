Questions, answered.

Can’t find it? Email hello@commonhouse.work.

Is there a minimum term?

No. Every membership is month-to-month with 30 days’ notice. Suites for 16+ can lock a 12-month rate for 8% off, but it’s never required.

Can I work from any building?

Yes. Roam members use any open desk at all six buildings. Anchor and Studio members have a home base but can drop into any other location, any day.

What’s actually included in the price?

Rent, utilities, fibre, daily cleaning, café drinks, 200 printed pages a month, your meeting-room allowance, member events and a staffed front desk. Extra meeting hours are billed at cost, and that’s it.

How quickly can my team move in?

Hot desks are same-day. Dedicated desks take 24 hours, and furnished studios are usually ready within 48 hours of signing. Suites need two to four weeks for branding and IT.

Do you offer day passes?

Yes: $29 for a day or $250 for a 10-pack that never expires. Day passes include coffee and one hour in a meeting room.

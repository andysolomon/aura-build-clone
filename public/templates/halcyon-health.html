Talk to our team

Good questions

Still curious? Our care navigators answer the phone in under a minute: (503) 555-0142.

Do you take my insurance?

We’re in-network with most major plans in Oregon and Washington, including Medicare. Your membership fee is separate and HSA/FSA eligible.

What if I need a specialist?

Your clinician refers you to a vetted specialist, shares your records and follows up afterward so nothing falls through the cracks.

Can I switch clinicians?

Anytime, no explanation needed. Fit matters, and we’ll help you find the right person.

Is there a contract?

No. Membership is month-to-month and you can pause or cancel from the app in two taps.

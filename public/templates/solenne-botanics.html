        Is Solenne suitable for sensitive skin?

        Every formula is dermatologist-tested on sensitive skin and made without synthetic fragrance or drying alcohols. Essential oils never exceed 0.5%, and our calendula oil was designed for reactive skin first.

        How does the glass refill program work?

        Send back any five empty vessels with our prepaid label. We sterilize and reuse every one and credit $10 to your account. Cleanser and body wash refills ship in compostable pouches.

        How long does a bottle last?

        Used daily, a 30 ml oil or serum lasts 10–12 weeks and a 150 ml cleanser about three months. Each batch is dated on the base; use within a year of opening.

        What if it isn’t right for my skin?

        Live with it for 60 days. If you don’t love it, we’ll refund you in full, even if the bottle is empty.

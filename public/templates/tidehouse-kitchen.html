    Good to know

    Before you come aboard.

    Do you take walk-ins?

    Always. The eight-seat oyster counter is walk-in only every night we’re open. Arrive before six for the best chance, or put your name down and wait with a drink on the deck.

    Can you cook around allergies?

    Tell us when you book. Our kitchen handles shellfish, fish, gluten, dairy and nuts, so we can’t promise a trace-free plate, but we will always cook around an allergy.

    Is there anything for non-seafood eaters?

    Every section has vegetarian dishes marked V, plus a dry-aged rib for two. A garden tasting menu is available with 48 hours’ notice.

    Where do I park?

    Harbour Row has free street parking after 6 p.m. Better still, take the train to Harbour Row station or the Sister Isle ferry, which docks right beside us.

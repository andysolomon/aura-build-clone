FAQ

Before you
walk in.

I’ve never lifted a barbell. Is this for me?

That’s who most of our members were. Everyone starts with Foundations: six small-group sessions on the squat, hinge, press and pull before joining regular classes. You’ll start with a PVC pipe if that’s what your body needs.

What should I bring to my first session?

Flat shoes, water and clothes you can squat in. We have chalk, belts, lifting shoes in most sizes, towels and lockers. Showers are on site.

How big are the classes?

Barbell Club caps at 14, Olympic Lifting at 10 and Engine at 18. Every class has a dedicated coach, and busy times get a second one on the floor.

Can I pause my membership?

Yes. Freeze for up to eight weeks a year for travel, injury or life, at no cost. Coached members keep their program and 1:1 slots on hold.

Is there parking?

Twenty free spaces behind the warehouse, secure bike racks inside, and the Eastgate tram stop is a four-minute walk.

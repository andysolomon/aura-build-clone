FAQ

Before you ship.

Still unsure? Our engineers answer in the community forum, usually within the hour.

Which frameworks does Forge support?

Forge auto-detects more than 40 frameworks and static site generators. Anything that produces static files, a long-running server, or Request/Response handlers deploys without configuration.

How do instant rollbacks work?

Builds are immutable and stay cached in every region for 30 days. A rollback just repoints traffic, so it completes globally in under a second with no rebuild.

Can I keep data in a specific region?

Yes. Pin functions and Edge KV to EU-only or US-only region groups per project, while static assets stay cached globally. Enterprise adds contractual data residency.

Do I need to rewrite my app for the edge?

Usually not. Standard runtime APIs cover most libraries, and anything that truly needs a long-running process can run in our regional containers right beside the edge.

What happens if I go over my plan?

Nothing breaks. Extra requests are billed at $2 per million and bandwidth at $0.06 per GB. Spend limits and alerts let you cap it hard if you prefer.

(03) Approach

Four questions we ask of every site.

  1. i.

    Where does the light arrive?

    We track the sun across the site for a full year before placing a single wall.

  2. ii.

    What was here before?

    Old walls, trees and paths are kept, reused or remembered in the plan.

  3. iii.

    What will age well?

    Lime, stone, timber and concrete — materials that look better at fifty than at five.

  4. iv.

    What can we leave out?

    Every element must earn its place. The best detail is often the one you remove.
